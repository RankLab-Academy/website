Before you start: four academy rules

1. Build real websites

Every site needs a brand, an audience, original tools, an editorial policy, named authors and a reason to exist. Thin affiliate sites are not part of this curriculum.

2. Never build a fake link network

Cross-links between your own sites must be contextual and useful. No sitewide footer rings, no exact-match anchor swaps, no duplicated content.

3. YMYL topics need extra trust

Finance, insurance, health, legal, loans and education ROI require expert review, official sources, disclaimers, update dates and transparent methodology.

4. AI is an assistant, not the expert

Use AI for briefs, outlines and editing. Never invent authors, reviews, testing, screenshots, data or credentials.
