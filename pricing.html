Read this before anything else: the checkout is not real

This is a static, front-end-only demonstration site. There is no server, no payment provider and no account. When you press an unlock button, a flag is written to IndexedDB in your own browser and nothing is charged or transmitted. Anyone can read every lesson in the page source.

A production version of this ladder must put a real payment provider and server-side entitlement checks in front of paid lessons. Client-side gating is a user-experience pattern, never a security boundary. This page says so plainly because the academy's own editorial policy forbids implying a capability the build does not have.

Why the price escalates

1. Price tracks the cost of the mistake

Getting Course 2 wrong costs you a few weeks in a bad niche. Getting Course 11 wrong can mean an FTC disclosure problem, a clawed-back affiliate account or a single sponsor who is 60% of revenue walking away. The later courses are cheap relative to what they prevent.

2. It enforces the order

A flat price invites people to jump to the monetisation course on day one and build a business on a niche that a generated answer will erase. The ladder makes the cheapest path the correct path: decide, then validate, then build, then monetise.

3. The free tier is genuinely enough to say no

Courses 0 and 1 exist so you can reach a defensible no without paying. If the 12-month cost model and the zero-click survival scorecard tell you the project fails, you have saved money and you owe us nothing. That is the honest outcome for a large share of readers.

Governing question

If Google disappeared tomorrow, why would anyone still come back to this brand? Every paid course on this ladder has to move that answer forward. If a course cannot, it should not be in the ladder — and the Month 3, 6 and 9 pruning gates apply to our own curriculum too.

Pricing questions

Do I have to buy the courses in order?

No, but the ladder is designed so that buying out of order is both more expensive and less useful. Course 11 assumes you already have the niche score from Course 2 and the cost model from Course 0.

What happens to my unlock if I clear my browser data?

In this demonstration build, it is gone — entitlements live in IndexedDB with everything else. Export a backup from the dashboard first. In a production build this would be tied to a server-side account instead.

Is there a refund policy?

There is no checkout, so there is nothing to refund. If this were a live product, the policy that matches the ladder would be a full refund within 14 days on any single course, with the free tier serving as the trial.

Why is Course 12 the most expensive when it has the same six lessons?

Because it decides what you shut down. The capstone runs the Month 3, 6 and 9 pruning gates across three live sites and forces a keep/prune/kill call on each. Capital-allocation decisions are where the money in this whole curriculum actually is.

Are the prices claims that expire?

Prices are our own commercial terms, not third-party claims, so Rule 1's 90-day claim expiry does not apply to them. Every external platform, CPM or compliance statement in the courses does carry a 90-day expiry and an owner in the claim register.
