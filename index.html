The problem this academy solves

Traffic without revenue

Most beginners chase volume keywords in low-value niches. You will learn to select topics where advertisers compete, and to match each page type to the monetisation that actually pays.

Content that cannot rank

Generic AI articles, thin affiliate reviews and rewritten competitor posts fail. You will build content with information gain: original tools, tables, screenshots, methodology and expert review.

One-time visitors

Search sends people once. Newsletters, calculators, trackers and saved checklists bring them back — the difference between a blog and a media asset.

Read this before you build 10 websites

If ten sites exist mainly to link to each other, search engines may treat that as a link scheme. This academy teaches the safe alternative: ten independent brands with their own audiences, authors and editorial policies, connected only by contextual links that genuinely help a reader. See the cross-referral safety rules.

This is a static site. There is no login, no server database and no analytics profile of you. Everything you do — completed lessons, notes, quiz scores, checklists, saved keywords, portfolio rows — is written to IndexedDB inside your own browser.

Frequently asked questions

Is the academy really free?

Yes. It is funded by display advertising and clearly labelled affiliate links, exactly like the business model it teaches. See our advertiser disclosure.

Do I need a domain or budget to start?

No. Courses 1, 2, 5, 6, 9, 10 and 11 can be completed with free tools. You need a domain (roughly $10–15/yr) and a Cloudflare account (free tier) from Course 3 onwards.

Will you teach me to buy backlinks?

No. Course 8 covers earned links only: original data, calculators, templates, expert commentary and digital PR. Paid links, PBNs and reciprocal rings are covered as risks to avoid.

Does this replace professional advice?

No. Several blueprint niches are YMYL (finance, insurance, legal, health). We teach the editorial process — expert review, official sources, disclaimers — not the advice itself.

Can I use the tools on my own site?

Yes. The calculators are plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Read the code, adapt it, and ship your own version as a linkable asset.
